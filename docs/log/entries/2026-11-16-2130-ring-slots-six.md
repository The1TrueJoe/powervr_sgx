# Six ring slots by default: a frame of four renders was one frame deep at four

2026-11-16, a CE5300 board with an installed 7.3.3 image (powervr_sgx 1.0.0), dev Mesa
de4d39c9. Minecraft 1.7.10 in the world at render distance 3, with a capture overlay. Each
captured frame is a `glBlitFramebufferEXT` from the window into a 360x240 texture, a pack pass
into a 90x360 FBO that samples it four times a fragment, and a `glReadPixels` of an older FBO
of a three-FBO ring. 20 s windows, `SGX_DEBUG=stats`.

- The readback was not the cost: `SGX_DEBUG=sync` put its wait at 0.28 ms on average, and it
  never ended a render. Readback alone, of an FBO packed once: no cost.
- The blit alone and the pack alone (from a texture blitted once) cost 1-3% each. Both together
  cost about 30%: 18.5 → 13 fps, and the slot wait grew from 583 to 689-787 ms per 2 s.
- Each render takes one of a context's ring slots (`sgxws_ctx.slots`, default four). Minecraft
  alone makes two renders a frame, so two frames are in flight. Each capture part adds one
  render, and three a frame still leaves about 1.3 frames in flight. With both, four renders a
  frame leave one, and recording the next frame waits for the GPU's whole previous frame.

| `SGX_SLOTS` | baseline fps | blit + pack fps | slot wait per 2 s | GEM memory in the world |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 18.2 / 18.7 | 12.9 / 13.1 | 689-787 ms | 147,552 KiB |
| 6 | 18.5 / 18.9 | 17.1 / 17.8 | 0 | 186,112 KiB |
| 8 | 17.8 / 18.8 | 17.4 / 17.7 | 0 | 248,448 KiB |

At six, full capture with the readback runs 17.1 fps, and recording with x264 at 360x240 at
30 fps runs 17.2. Six is the default now; `SGX_SLOTS` still overrides (2-8). A slot is about
20 MB of rings per context (general ring 16 MB, two PDS rings 2 MB each, VDM 0.5 MB), so six
cost about 38 MB more per GL program than four.
