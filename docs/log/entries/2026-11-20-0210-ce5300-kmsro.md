# kmsro with the ce5300 display driver: the SGX found as a sibling, frames into its dumb buffers

2026-11-20, a CE5300 board, RAM-booted into a test kernel (7.3.3 with powervr_sgx, simpledrm
and the `ce5300` KMS driver), our Mesa (dev with the new patch 0004) staged into the
Debian root by hand.

**Display:** `ce5300` binds the display controller at `0000:01:08.0` (KMS only), with
`takeover=1` at runtime. It replaces simpledrm on CEFDK's 720x480 and becomes `card2`.
In sysfs it sits beside the SGX (`0000:01:02.0`, `card1` + `renderD128`), under the same
bridge (`0000:00:01.0`).

**Before the change:** kmsro took a render node only from a child of a non-platform display
device (gma500's auxiliary SGX), so nothing paired with `ce5300`.

**Run:** `mesa/tests/sgx_kmscube -d /dev/dri/card2 -n 3000 -a 0` (GBM surface, XRGB8888,
`drmModeSetCrtc` a frame):
- GBM's backend is drm, `GL_RENDERER` is PowerVR SGX545: the sibling lookup gave
  powervr_sgx's render node.
- 3,000 frames at 289 fps.
- The two framebuffers on `ce5300` are 720x480 XR24 with a pitch of 2944 (736 px, the
  render target's 32-pixel width), GEM refcount 4. `ce5300`'s GEM DMA import takes only
  contiguous memory, so these are its own dumb buffers, imported into powervr_sgx and
  rendered into directly.
- The same image on simpledrm (`card0`, before the takeover): 102 fps, and the
  scanout check at 0x7fc00000 is right.

**Not checked:**
- The pixels of `ce5300`'s scanout (the check reads a fixed physical address; the dumb
  buffers' addresses aren't known from userspace).
- 720p and 1080p.
- X or a Wayland compositor.

**Kernel (ce5300, not this change):** every client exit WARNs `atomic remove_fb failed
with -22` (drm_framebuffer.c, from `drm_mode_rmfb_work_fn`): removing the framebuffer still
on the CRTC commits a plane disable that the driver's atomic check refuses. Reported to the
display driver's work.
