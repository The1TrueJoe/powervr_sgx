# Eight clip planes in the TA, and points that keep their size

2026-11-26, a CE5300 board (SGX545 rev 1014, open microkernel 0.2.1), our Mesa in the Debian
root, surfaceless EGL, desktop GL 2.1 (`SGX_DEBUG=glsl130` and a 3.0 compatibility context
for GLSL 1.30's distances).

The native path (the compiler's `fdpc` into cp<n>, output select b0-5) has been in since
2026-11-11, and it works on this board. Issue #5 reports unclipped planes on an N2600
(rev 10131), which this run can't check. `mesa/tests/sgx_ucp.c` passes plane 0 on a quad, a strip, a transformed plane, a line, points,
an FBO and gl_ClipVertex. That settles STATE.md's open question: the result lands in
o<clip_o + p>. Two things were wrong.

**Planes 6 and 7:** GLSL 1.30 makes `GL_MAX_CLIP_PLANES` 8, but the driver enabled only b0-5,
so planes 6 and 7 were ignored. Output select b6 and b7 enable cp6 and cp7. With them, plane
6, plane 7 and `gl_ClipDistance[7]` clip. Without them, all three drew both halves.

**Points with a clip plane:** output select b8 takes the last output dword as the point size,
and the clip results came after it. A 1 px point under a plane at distance 4 was drawn 4 px
wide (64 and 56 white pixels for 4 and 4 points). The compiler now puts the clip results after
the sets and the point size last. The points are 1 px again, and the row of points under plane
0 is 16 of 16 (it was 15).

| `sgx_ucp` | dev | this change |
|---|---|---|
| quad, strip, ortho, line, plane5, fbo, glsl | pass | pass |
| points | 15 of 16 (pass) | 16 of 16 |
| plane6, plane7, clip7 (glsl130) | fail: not clipped | pass |
| psize (points under a plane) | fail: 4 px points | pass |

**Tests:**
- `sgx_ucp`, all 12 cases, three runs in a row; `sgx_clipfbo` 0 of 20 rounds wrong;
  `sgx_occlusion` 17 of 17.
- piglit, the clipping, occlusion and conditional-render tests (1,470 results), against dev
  (78d0655b): no change. `fixed-clip-enables` fails about half the time on dev and on this
  change alike (11 and 12 of 20). Plane 2 isn't applied when the enables go from 0, 1, 4, 5 to
  0-3, so it moves to the flakes list. 104 clipping entries in `sgx-piglit-fails.txt`
  (`clipflat`, `user-clip`, `vs-clip-vertex-*`) pass on dev and come out of the list.
- Full dEQP-GLES2, with this change and conditional render together: 16,644 pass, 70 expected
  fails, 446 skips, nothing new failing. Each result is the same as in the 0.2.3 gate run on dev's CI
  Mesa (2026-11-24, this board), including 5 passes the older baseline lists as fails
  (`polygon_offset.fixed16_render_with_factor`, `texture.mipmap.2d.bias.*`).
