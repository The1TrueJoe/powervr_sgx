# 384 vertex uniform vectors, and the driver's constants moved past them

2026-11-25, a CE5300 board (SGX545 rev 1014, the 7.3.3 kernel it boots, open microkernel v0.2.1),
our Mesa in the Debian root, surfaceless EGL, GLES2. Issue #5: reVC (Grand Theft Auto: Vice City
on librw's gl3 backend) crashes as the game starts. A tester's gdb log on Cedarview shows the
link of librw's skinning shader failing:
- "Too many vertex shader default uniform block components";
- "Too many vertex shader uniform components".

librw gets no program back and dereferences it.

**Cause:** librw's skin shader declares 64 bone matrices (256 vec4), the projection, view and
world matrices, 8 lights of 4 vec4, and a few vectors, about 304 vec4 in a vertex program. The
driver allowed 256 + 9 (st's clip planes and point size). The limit couldn't simply be raised:
- The driver's own constants were numbered from 1000: texture sizes, gl_InstanceID, the
  native-integer block, UBO addresses and level counts.
- A uniform is numbered by its dword in constant buffer 0, so the vertex range (1060 dwords)
  already overlapped them by 15 vec4.

**Change:**
- `GL_MAX_VERTEX_UNIFORM_VECTORS` is now 384 (`SGX_MAX_VS_UNIFORMS`, plus st's 9), 1572 dwords.
- The driver's constants are numbered from 2024, with the same layout, and `SGX_MAX_CONST_K` is
  2304. `sgx_context.h` checks at compile time that the block clears the uniforms.
- Constants past sa127 already go to the memory block (ld.a.d, one 128-word window a base), so
  the only other change is the window count: 12 to 16, enough for 1572 dwords plus the driver's
  constants and the literals.
- Fragment programs keep 128 vec4.

**Tests:**
- `mesa/tests/sgx_skin`: our own skinning shader with librw's uniform footprint (64 mat4 bones
  indexed per vertex, three mat4, 8 lights of 4 vec4, four vectors). The quad goes through bone
  63 alone and is lit by light 7 alone.
  - dev: the link fails with the tester's two messages.
  - This change: `GL_MAX_VERTEX_UNIFORM_VECTORS` 384, and every pixel of the 32x32 target is right.
- `mesa/tests/sgx_vconst` (a vertex vec4 array as large as the limit, indexed by an attribute;
  constant indices; a mat4 array; one vec4 past the limit must fail to link) passes at 256 on dev
  and at 384 with this change.
- Full dEQP-GLES2: 16,649 pass, 70 expected fails, 446 skips, the same as dev, with nothing new
  failing.
- piglit's uniform-limit tests, dev against this change:
  - `built-in constants` (`gl_MaxVertexUniformComponents` and the rest) and
    `getuniformlocation-array-of-struct-of-array` pass on both. Their expectations dated from a
    120-vec4 limit and are dropped.
  - `big-param` (1021 ARB program parameters), `glsl-vs-large-uniform-array` (a vertex
    vec4[500]) and `glsl-fs-uniform-array-7` (fragment) are still over the limits and still fail.
