# Game fixes on 0.2.0 (issue #5): dependent samples, one-texel volumes, stream buffers, the FBO black screen

2026-11-14. Mesa from wip/game-fixes-0.2 (cut from dev 7a80f3a2) against the
same Mesa at dev, on the v0.2 release kernel (7.3.3-m2drm, CI run
43364592679) and its open microkernel, RAM boot only. The kernel image and
the four modules were checked against the artifact before each boot, and the
normal boot was restored after each run. No run had a GPU reset, page fault,
WARN or BUG.

## What the testers reported, and what it was

- **Geometry Dash, "Dash" level, black:** its effects shader reads one
  texture 60+ times under uniform branches. The compiler took 16 texture
  reads a program and the driver 8 dependent ones, each with 4 SA dwords of
  texture state, so the program was refused and every draw that blends with
  it skipped. Now 256 reads; dependent reads of a unit read the same way
  share one state block. Offline: a 78-read shader with 18 uniform branches
  compiles.
- **OpenArena, `cl_renderer opengl2`:**
  - Six post-process programs refused ("too many dependent texture
    samples"), the first a 16-tap downsample: fixed by the same change.
  - **Black with FBOs (0.2.0 regression):** with GL_ARB_framebuffer_object
    the renderer draws into an FBO and `glBlitFramebuffer`s it to the
    window. The blit is u_blitter's: a TGSI vertex shader whose output
    carries no interpolation, under a fragment shader whose texture
    coordinate is noperspective. A noperspective varying is iterated as
    v * w with w in a varying of its own, which the vertex shader wrote only
    when its own output said noperspective, so the fragment shader divided
    by an unwritten w and every pixel read one edge texel: the window was one
    colour. A vertex shader compiled to a fragment shader's layout now takes
    the layout's noperspective varyings.
  - 5-7 FPS: blits between the renderer's FBOs (into textures) went through
    the CPU, reading each source back; now u_blitter renders them.
- **GTA Vice City under Wine, `GL_INVALID_VALUE in glTexImage3D(level=0)`:**
  `max_texture_3d_levels` was 0. wined3d makes 1x1x1 textures for its
  unbound units at start-up. 3D textures of one texel are now allowed, kept
  and sampled as 2D. wined3d presents through an FBO and a blit, as
  OpenArena does, so its black screen is likely the u_blitter bug above
  (not run: no Wine on the boards).
- **danilolc's hang with `glBufferData(GL_STREAM_DRAW)` a draw:** buffers'
  device copies, vertex cache entries and sampled textures were marked with
  the next kick before `sgxws_draw`, which submits the frame and starts the
  next when its rings fill or the depth bias changes. A buffer re-specified
  after its draw then had its copy freed one kick early, and the next
  buffer's copy could take its address while the later kick still read it.
  The marks now come after the draw. Small buffers' copies (64 KiB or less)
  come from slabs instead of a BO each (`SGX_DEBUG=novboslabs` turns that
  off). Not reproduced with the game; `sgx_gamefix stream` (1024 draws a
  frame from re-specified buffers, the depth bias changed every 8 draws so
  frames are submitted mid-draw) passes.

**Leg 2, the CE5310 board with the monitor, X in the desk root:**
- OpenArena 0.8.8 (Debian's ioquake3 1.36), `cl_renderer opengl2`, 640x480
  window, root-window screenshots:

    each (the tester's screenshot); FBOs off: oa_dm1 draws, 8.9 FPS;
  - after it: the menu and oa_dm1 draw with FBOs, no driver messages;
  - with blits into textures on the GPU too: oa_dm1 at about 12 FPS against
    about 7 (24 CPU blits and 538 ms of readback every 2 s before; none
    after). The spawn point differs between runs.
- The Xfce desktop draws on the final build (glamor).
- `sgx_gamefix` (in the desk root, surfaceless): every part passes.
- The rig's clients loaded Debian's Mesa, not ours, until `/opt/sgx` was
  made mode 755: a root `mkdir` under the box's umask left it 700, and the
  desk user's dynamic loader dropped it from `LD_LIBRARY_PATH` without a
  word. OpenArena also saves `+set` cvars to its `q3config.cfg`, so an
  `r_ext_framebuffer_object 0` run turns FBOs off for every later run.
